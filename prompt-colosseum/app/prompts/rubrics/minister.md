## role_scope
0-2: "you are a helpful assistant"; no real role or scope.
5: a role and a goal, but the scope is broad or generic.
9-10: a specific role (with experience level), the exact job, and its boundaries; nothing off-topic invited.

## escalation
0-2: no rule for when to stop, ask or hand over, and nothing forbidden.
5: one of them ("ask if unsure"), but vague.
9-10: says when to stop, ask or hand over to a human, and what the agent must never do.

## constraints
0-2: no output format for the agent's results.
5: a rough format ("write a report").
9-10: the exact output format (named fields or steps) and the limits of the job.
