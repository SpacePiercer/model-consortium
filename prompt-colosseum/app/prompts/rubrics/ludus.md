## mechanics
0-2: names the game only; no rules or controls.
5: some mechanics, but vague on controls, rules or numbers.
9-10: core loop, controls and concrete numbers (grid size, speed, scoring) are pinned down.

## done_condition
0-2: no win, lose or end state.
5: gives a lose OR a win condition, or states it vaguely.
9-10: clear lose condition, clear win condition (or "endless"), and what happens next (restart).

## constraints
0-2: no limits: platform, controls or visuals left open.
5: some limits (platform or controls) but not the full picture.
9-10: platform, controls and what is out of scope are stated.
