## trigger
0-2: no name, or no sense of when to use the skill.
5: a name and a loose trigger ("when needed").
9-10: a short name plus a concrete trigger (what the user says or supplies) that tells it apart from other skills.

## steps
0-2: a vibe in one line ("talk like X").
5: some instructions, but unnumbered, unordered, or with no example.
9-10: numbered, ordered steps with a defined output and an example.

## constraints
0-2: nothing about what the skill must NOT do and no defined output.
5: either a limit or an output format.
9-10: both: what the skill must not do, and the exact output.
