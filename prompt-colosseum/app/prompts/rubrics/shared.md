## clarity
0-2: the goal is unclear or contradicts itself; the reader must guess what is wanted.
5: the goal is clear, but vague words ("nice", "good", "great") leave obvious gaps.
9-10: one clear goal; nothing left to guess.

## constraints
0-2: no scope, limits or output format at all.
5: some limits or a format, but incomplete.
9-10: scope, limits and the exact output format are all stated.

## economy
0-2: padding, repetition or keyword soup.
5: some filler, but the point lands.
9-10: every word earns its place.
