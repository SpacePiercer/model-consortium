## likeness
0-2: describes a different scene, subject or medium (a photo prompt for a painting).
5: right subject, but composition, colours, light or medium are off or missing.
9-10: a generator given this would reproduce subject, composition, colours, lighting and medium.

## specificity
0-2: generic ("a nice landscape").
5: a few concrete details, mixed with vague or wrong ones.
9-10: many checkable details that really appear in the image (named colours, objects, placement, time of day); none invented.

## constraints
0-2: no framing, style or negative prompt.
5: style or framing named, or a negative prompt, but not both.
9-10: subject, style and framing stated, plus a negative prompt that excludes what the image does not contain.
